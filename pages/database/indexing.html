Indexing — Fast Lookups at a Cost

Indexes are shortcuts that make reads fast. B-trees turn linear scans into logarithmic lookups. But every index slows down writes. Choose wisely.

The Tradeoff

Read Speed vs Write Cost

Without an index, finding a record is O(n) — scan every row. With a B-tree index, it is O(log n). But every insert, update, or delete must update the index. Choose your indexes carefully.

  • B-tree index: ordered, range queries work, common default.
  • Hash index: fast equality lookups, no range queries.
  • Composite index: index on multiple columns, careful ordering matters.
  • Partial index: index only rows matching a condition (smaller, faster).
